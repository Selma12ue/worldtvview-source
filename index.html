WATCH IN YOUR BROWSER

Video Player

Enter an HTTP/HTTPS video URL, including a link that redirects to MP4, HLS (.m3u8), or MPEG-TS (.ts). Browsers follow standard HTTP redirects automatically, but the final response must be a playable media stream. Playback depends on browser support: HLS works natively in some browsers, and direct TS playback is limited. Ordinary website pages are not playable.

Use a direct, browser-supported video link. HTTP streams may be blocked on secure pages.
